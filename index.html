About

A little about me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Physics at Virginia Tech, working with Prof. Sophia Economou and Prof. Tianci Zhou. My current projects span diffusion in U(1)-charge-conserving dynamics, early fault-tolerant algorithms for multi-point time-dependent correlators, and Hessian recycling in Floquet-ADAPT-VQE.

Before Virginia Tech, I completed my M.Sc. in Physics at IIT Madras under Prof. Arul Lakshminarayan, where I studied random quantum circuits through the lens of random matrix theory, and stayed on as a Junior Research Fellow working on the spectra of random quantum channels. I did my B.Sc. in Physics at Banaras Hindu University.

I am broadly interested in how quantum many-body systems thermalize — or fail to — and in using tools from quantum information and quantum chaos to understand it.

Research

Current & past projects

Ph.D. Research · Virginia Tech · Ongoing
Early Fault-Tolerant Algorithms for Multi-Point Time-Dependent Correlators
with Prof. Sophia Economou & Prof. Ed Barnes

Extending the SPICE-Q framework to disordered quantum systems, with a focus on resource-efficient disorder averaging and multi-point time-dependent correlators. Manuscript in preparation; expected on arXiv shortly.

Ph.D. Research · Virginia Tech · Ongoing
Accurate Determination of Diffusion Constants through Channel Evolution of U(1)-Charge-Conserving Hamiltonians
with Prof. Tianci Zhou

Studying diffusion and charge transport in symmetry-constrained interacting quantum many-body systems, combining analytical ideas and numerics to extract transport coefficients from long-time dynamics.

Ph.D. Research · Virginia Tech · Ongoing
Hessian Recycling in Floquet-ADAPT-VQE
with Prof. Sophia Economou

Carrying approximate second-order information across iterations of Floquet-ADAPT-VQE as the ansatz grows, to get faster, more stable convergence and cut measurement and optimizer overhead when simulating periodically driven systems.

Long-Term Visiting Student · ICTS-TIFR Bangalore
Partial Spectral Form Factors in Random Quantum Circuits near Localization
with Prof. Sthitadhi Roy

Using the partial spectral form factor to probe eigenstate correlations in Floquet random unitary circuits across the ergodic-to-MBL transition, combining analytics in the ergodic phase with large-scale numerics near the transition.

Junior Research Fellow · IIT Madras · Jul – Dec 2024
Random Quantum Channels and Associated Spectrum
with Prof. Arul Lakshminarayan

Spectral statistics of quantum channels built from permutations: showed how the number of peripheral eigenvalues depends on the permutation's cycle structure, derived the dependence of entangling power on the permutation, and obtained the Single Ring Theorem in certain cases.

M.Sc. Thesis · IIT Madras · 2024
Random Quantum Circuits and Random Matrix Theory
Advisor: Prof. Arul Lakshminarayan

Observed a localization-to-delocalization transition (Poisson → Wigner-Dyson) in brickwork Floquet circuits, using the average level-spacing ratio as order parameter and entangling power as control parameter.

Summer Intern · IIT Palakkad · 2023
Quantum Phase Transitions in the Transverse-Field Ising Model
with Prof. Uma Divakaran

Jordan-Wigner fermionization and a reproduction study of quantum phase transitions in a spatially modulated field.
